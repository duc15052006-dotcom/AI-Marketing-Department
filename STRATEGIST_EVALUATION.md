## Canonical identity contract

The AI Marketing Department has exactly five permanent ASIs:

1. CMO
2. Intelligence
3. Content
4. Creative
5. Performance

The former Strategist role has been decomposed across the canonical five-agent authority model:

- **CMO** owns marketing strategy, positioning, GTM choices, commercial trade-offs, budget/resource allocation, and final commercial sign-off.
- **Content** owns evidence-grounded messaging architecture, copy/scripts, editorial planning, SEO content briefing, lifecycle messaging, and channel-native content adaptation.
- **Intelligence** owns market/customer/competitor research and evidence acquisition.
- **Creative** owns visual/multimedia concepts and production.
- **Performance** owns authoritative measurement, attribution, paid execution analysis, and observed performance outcomes.

Historical serialized identifiers such as `STRATEGIST`, `strategist`, or `STRATEGY` may be accepted only at compatibility boundaries and must normalize immediately to canonical `CONTENT` where the compatibility contract requires it. They do not grant independent runtime, model-policy, governance, memory, tool, or approval authority.
