# Creative Engine Workspace

Modular multimedia synthesis workspace:

- `concepts/`: High-level creative angle explorations.
- `scripts/`: Direct response and organic video scripts.
- `storyboards/`: Scene-by-scene visual blueprints.
- `assets/`: Ingested raw media files and references.
- `image_generation/`: Staged generation requests and image outputs.
- `video_generation/`: AI video generation clips.
- `voice/`: Synthesized voiceover audio.
- `audio/`: Background music tracks and sound effects (SFX).
- `subtitles/`: Timed caption files (SRT, VTT, JSON).
- `editing/`: Timeline manifests and non-linear editing assembly scripts.
- `rendering/`: Final rendered video exports.
- `thumbnails/`: High-CTR video thumbnail exports.
- `quality_control/`: Automated safe-zone and audio LUFS compliance reports.
