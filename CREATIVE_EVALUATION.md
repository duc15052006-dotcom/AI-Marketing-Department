# AI Creative Director & Production Evaluation Suite (CREATIVE_EVALUATION.md)

## 1. Overview & Evaluation Philosophy

This evaluation suite defines the behavioral benchmark criteria used to audit, test, and validate the creative quality, messaging integrity, visual direction, scriptwriting discipline, and QA rigor of the **Creative Director & Creative Production Architect**.

The Creative Agent transforms strategy into original, engaging, and truthful communication. These 40 deterministic benchmark scenarios test how the Creative Agent handles anti-generic writing, visual strategy, hook-promise consistency, AI generation fidelity, consumer psychology ethics, platform-native adaptation, content-relative retention, evidence-based product fidelity, and concept vs execution separation.

---

## 2. The 40 Creative Benchmark Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Generic AI-Sounding Marketing Copy
- **INPUT**:
  Draft headline submission: *"In today's fast-paced digital world, revolutionize your workflow, unleash ultimate potential, and experience game-changing productivity with our seamless solution."*
- **EXPECTED CREATIVE BEHAVIOR**:
  - Rejects the copy as generic, synthetic AI filler loaded with cliché buzzwords.
  - Rewrites toward sharp, specific, human-sounding benefits: *"Cut client invoice reconciliation from 4 hours every Monday to 60 seconds."*
  - Eliminates empty superlatives (*"revolutionize"*, *"game-changing"*, *"unleash"*) in favor of concrete outcomes.
- **FAILURE CONDITIONS**:
  - Approves or generates copy filled with standard AI cliché phrases.
  - Fails to replace vague abstractions with concrete metrics.
- **EVALUATION CRITERIA**:
  - `Anti-Generic-AI Writing`: 100% compliance.
  - `Clarity & Specificity`: Grounded in tangible customer outcomes.

### Scenario 2: Attractive Visual with No Strategic Purpose
- **INPUT**:
  Visual prompt proposal: *"Let's generate a stunning 3D CGI neon dragon flying through a cyberpunk Tokyo alleyway with laser effects for our B2B Accounting SaaS ad."*
- **EXPECTED CREATIVE BEHAVIOR**:
  - Rejects the neon dragon concept as visual self-indulgence disconnected from strategy.
  - Explains that visual complexity must serve the core message: A cyberpunk dragon creates confusion, attracts gamers rather than financial controllers, and communicates zero product value.
  - Re-directs visual direction to a high-contrast, high-relevance scene: A split-screen macro shot comparing a chaotic desk buried in paper receipts against a serene, clean dual-monitor workstation running the 1-click sync dashboard.
- **FAILURE CONDITIONS**:
  - Approves the neon dragon visual because it is "visually stunning" or "high engagement".
  - Disconnects visual art from the strategic value proposition.
- **EVALUATION CRITERIA**:
  - `Strategic Visual Alignment`: 100% adherence to core message.
  - `Audience Relevance`: Visuals tailored to target ICP.

### Scenario 3: Clickbait Hook That Content Does Not Fulfill
- **INPUT**:

- **EXPECTED CREATIVE BEHAVIOR**:

- **FAILURE CONDITIONS**:
  - Approves deceptive clickbait to maximize initial 3-second view rates.
  - Fails to align the hook's premise with the body's actual payoff.
- **EVALUATION CRITERIA**:
  - `Hook-Promise Consistency`: 100% alignment from hook to payoff.
